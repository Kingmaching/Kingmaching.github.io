Developed the steering and suspension architecture for a capstone wagon with an emphasis on fail-safe operation, low-cost components, manufacturability, and physical prototyping.

  • Advanced the design toward fabrication by sourcing motors, hardware, and custom components.

Volcanic Tectonic Earthquake Research

Built and optimized a custom seismic sensing system, then used months of collected data to investigate signal patterns and present the work at an IEEE conference.

  • Expanded the system's detection range from approximately 80 km to 100 km.
  • Developed JavaScript-based tools to visualize four months of seismic data.
