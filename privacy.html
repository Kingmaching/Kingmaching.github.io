Privacy & cookies

Last updated 29 October 2026

This is the personal portfolio of Marco Ching, an individual based in Hong Kong. It is not a business and sells nothing. I am responsible for the personal data described here. Contact: marcokingching@gmail.com.

The short version

  • No analytics, no advertising, no tracking pixels, no social-media widgets.
  • No cookies, and nothing stored in your browser.
  • The only personal data I receive is what you choose to send through the contact form or by email.

Who else handles it

  • Web3Forms receives the form when you press “Send message” and emails it to me. It may also keep a copy of submissions for a period set by its own policy. See the Web3Forms privacy policy.
  • Google (Gmail) holds my inbox, where messages arrive. See the Google privacy policy.
  • GitHub Pages hosts this site. Like any web server, it receives your IP address to deliver the pages, and GitHub logs and stores it for security purposes. I cannot see those logs. See the GitHub privacy statement.

These services may process data outside Hong Kong, for example in the United States.

Fonts and scripts are served from this site itself, so loading a page sends nothing to font or script providers. Links to my reports (Google Drive and Docs) and to LinkedIn open those sites, and their own privacy policies apply once you are there.

Cookies and browser storage

This site sets no cookies and uses no local storage, session storage or similar technology, and nor do the services above when you browse it. That is why there is no cookie banner: there is nothing to consent to. If that ever changes, this page will be updated first and a consent choice will be added where the law requires one.

The “Copy” button next to my email address writes only to your clipboard, at your request.

Your rights

Under Hong Kong’s Personal Data (Privacy) Ordinance (Cap. 486) you may ask for a copy of the personal data I hold about you and ask me to correct it. Depending on where you live (for example the EU or UK under the GDPR, or Canada under PIPEDA), you may also have rights to have it deleted, to object to its use, or to complain to your data protection authority. Whatever the law where you are, I will delete your messages on request. Email marcokingching@gmail.com; there is no charge.

Children

This site is not aimed at children. If you are under 13, please do not send personal information through the form.

Changes

If what this site collects changes, I will update this page and the date at the top.
